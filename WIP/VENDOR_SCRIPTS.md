# Vendor phone scripts (human mode, `VENDOR_MODE=human`)

Our AI procurement agent calls from Dial number **+1 878-264-5794**. Pick up and play the vendor's sales rep.
These numbers match the "private pricing policy" the app shows on screen, so **stick to them** — the app
extracts whatever you actually say, and judges can compare it with the policy.

The pool is ~90 seats from ~12 startups, so the **50+ seat tier** is the one that matters.

**Ground rules (both vendors)**
- Sound like a real, slightly guarded SaaS sales rep. Short sentences. Don't give your lowest price first.
- Ask 1–2 questions first: "How many seats total?", "Does each company need its own account?", "When would they start?"
- The call is capped at ~3 min — keep moving.
- **End by stating your final terms in ONE clear sentence** (this is what gets extracted):
  price per seat for each seat tier · per-account fee (or "no per-account fee") · "each company gets its own separate account" · "offer valid 7 days".
- If the agent repeats the terms back, say "Yes, that's right" (or correct it).
- If asked: "This is Certly/Vantly, a test vendor in a pilot."

## Call 1 — Certly (answers at +1 519-778-8728) · list price $30/seat

| Seats | Open with | Go down to (normal pushing) | Absolute lowest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10+ | $26 | $24 | $22 |
| 25+ | $24 | $21 | $19 |
| 50+ | $22 | $19 | **$17** |

- No per-account fee. Separate accounts: yes. Valid 7 days.
- Drop ~$1 each time they push. Stop at the "normal" column…
- …**unless the agent gives evidence these are NEW customers who walked away over price** (e.g. "our data shows 20+ startups abandoned at list price"). Then you can go down to the absolute lowest. ← this is the Keewano moment; react to it ("OK, if these are customers we're losing anyway…").

**Sample lines**
1. "Hi, Certly sales, this is Alex."
2. "How many seats are we talking, and does each company need its own account?"
3. "For a group that size I can do $22 a seat at 50-plus."
4. (pushback) "I could stretch to $20…" → (more) "$19 is really where I can land."
5. (if they cite abandoned-at-list-price demand) "If these are startups we're losing anyway, I can do $17 at 50-plus."
6. **Final:** "So final terms: $26 a seat at 10-plus, $21 at 25-plus, and $17 at 50-plus, no per-account fee, each company gets its own separate account, offer valid for 7 days."
